Buyer Verification Report

Already found a buyer in East Africa and want to be certain before you ship?

We visit that buyer on your behalf. We see their premises, assess whether there is genuine stock and storage, meet the owner, check trade references, ask other traders in that market whether they pay, and confirm the business actually operates as claimed.

You receive a written report with photographs and our honest assessment - including a recommendation not to proceed, where that is warranted.

Fee: USD 250 to 350 per buyer, depending on location and depth of verification.

Where an order proceeds following our verification, a commission applies on the shipment value, agreed in advance.

This is the lowest-risk way to begin working with us. No retainer, no long commitment - one buyer, one visit, one honest answer.
